An index that lives on your machine. 索引完全存储 在你的设备上。

[ 01 ]

Automatic tagging自动标签

Point TIOL at a folder and it automatically tags your images — subjects, scenes, colours, time of day. Keywords are written in a database. Your photos are never changed. 将 TIOL 指向一个文件夹，它会自动为你的图像添加标签——主题、场景、颜色、时段。关键词写入数据库。你的图像不会被修改。

[ 02 ]

Semantic search语义搜索

Search the way you remember. "Sunset Beach" works as a query — matching happens by meaning, not just file or folder names. Based on the SigLIP model. 用你记忆中的方式搜索。"夕阳沙滩" 作为一个查询词完全有效——匹配基于语义，而不仅仅是文件名或文件夹名。基于SigLIP模型工作。

Designed for photographers. 用户体验为本。

Appearance外观

Light & dark, done right浅色与深色，恰到好处

A calibrated dark theme for late-night culling sessions, and a paper-light mode for the studio. The entire interface follows your choice instantly — there is even a choice for Liquid Glass. 经过校准的深色主题适合深夜筛选，纸面浅色模式适合工作室环境。整个界面即时跟随你的选择，甚至还有液态玻璃风格开关。

Lens & camera metadata镜头与相机元数据

Filter your library by lens, camera body, or focal length. Need the compression of an 85mm, or everything shot wide at 24mm? It's one click — read straight from each file. 按镜头、相机机身或焦距筛选图库。需要 85mm 的压缩感，或者 24mm 广角拍摄的所有照片？只需一次点击——直接从每个文件中读取。
